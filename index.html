How to Play

1. Move & Look

WASD walk · Drag to look · F flashlight · SPACE climb hill · E use / grab / drop

2. Investigate

Click or tap objects to examine. Grab and move clues. Find enough clues before you can leave a level.

3. Use Every Tool

Hold food, water, medical supplies, the live map, or compass from your inventory. Open the field phone to decrypt messages, check your bearing, review clues, and contact witnesses.

4. Talk & Ask

When a character is in view, click to talk. Open the phone's Messages, Field Map, Compass, and Case File apps. Ask witnesses a question or tap a prompt; press ESC to put the phone away.

"Don't choose what the game lets you do.
Tell the game what you want to do."

Play as a Team

Take turns at one keyboard and build a shared case together. Every teammate can explore, question witnesses, and add discoveries to the team's evidence board.

LOCAL CO-OP · ONE DEVICEPass-the-controller teamwork. No accounts, servers, or private network needed.
🧭Field ScoutFind landmarks and examine the scene.

🔬Evidence AnalystConnect clues and compare timelines.

All investigators share one world and evidence board, with story progress carried into the next level. Online matchmaking is not enabled.
